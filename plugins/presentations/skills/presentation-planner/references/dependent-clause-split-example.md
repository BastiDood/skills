# Dependent Clause Split

<original_verbatim_text>

The general theme is that skill tradeoffs must be made. If we are to embrace these AI tools and fully extract their value in the workplace, then we must be prepared to let go of incidental skills that hinder the work our role values.

</original_verbatim_text>

<expected_on_slide_content>

- The general theme is that **skill tradeoffs must be made.**
  - If we are to embrace these **AI tools** and fully extract their value in the workplace,
    - then we must be prepared to let go of **incidental skills** that hinder the work our role values.

</expected_on_slide_content>

<expected_presenter_notes>

- The general theme is that skill tradeoffs must be made.
  - If we are to embrace these AI tools and fully extract their value in the workplace,
    - then we must be prepared to let go of incidental skills that hinder the work our role values.

</expected_presenter_notes>

<rule_demonstrated>

The second sentence supports the opening conclusion and becomes level 1. Its condition and consequence are easier to deliver separately, so the comma becomes the split boundary and remains attached to the condition. The lowercase `then` remains unchanged. Because the consequence completes the condition rather than starting a new move, it becomes level 2.

</rule_demonstrated>
